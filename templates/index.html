📈 Track Record — Does This Tool's Signal Actually Work?

Corrected 2 Nov 2026. The previous version of this page overstated results (it used price moves from before some signals, counted "wins" where the entry price was never reached, and counted repeat signals as separate trades). Every signal has been re-scored with the method below; the old figures are shown in grey for transparency only.
How each signal is scored: a signal is logged when a symbol newly reaches STRONG BUY, or a held position newly enters a BUY zone. Entry = the opening price of the first trading session after the day the signal was logged (so no price from before the alert is ever used; sometimes a day later than you could have bought). Exit = the signal's stop or target, whichever is reached first; a gap through either exits at the open; if both are touched on the same day it counts as the stop (daily prices can't show which came first); after 30 days, the last close. If the entry open is already beyond the stop or target the setup is skipped. Only one trade per stock at a time — repeat signals while a trade is open are excluded. Returns are before fees, slippage and tax. Not financial advice.
