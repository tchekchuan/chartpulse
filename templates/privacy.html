Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: August 16, 2026

getChartPulse is a personal project built and operated by one person (not a company). This page describes exactly what the site collects and why, in plain language — no legal boilerplate you can't verify against what the code actually does.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you subscribe for STRONG BUY alerts or log in to "My Portfolio." Confirmed via a double opt-in link before anything is stored as active.
  • Symbols you add to "My Portfolio" — stock tickers you choose to track, tied to your email, so we can show your own ratings and correlation view.
  • A login session cookie — set only after you click a login link or enter a login code, so you stay signed in. It's not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Standard web server logs (IP address, request timing) — kept briefly by our hosting provider for abuse prevention (e.g. rate-limiting repeated requests), not tied to your identity beyond that.

We do not collect passwords (there aren't any — login is via emailed link/code only), payment information, or any financial account details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your email or portfolio data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or ad networks.
  • We don't look at your portfolio symbols for anything other than showing them back to you.

Who else touches this data

A few third-party services process data on our behalf, strictly to run the site:

  • Resend — sends the emails (alerts, login links, confirmations).
  • Neon (Postgres) — stores subscriber emails, portfolio symbols, and login sessions.
  • Render — hosts the application itself.
  • Yahoo Finance / Finnhub — provide the stock market data shown on the site. We don't send them any information about you.

None of these are permitted to use your data for their own purposes beyond providing the service to us.

How to delete your data

Every alert email has an unsubscribe link that immediately removes your email from our subscriber list. To also delete your saved portfolio symbols or login history, or if you have any other request, reply to any alert email or reach out to Shawn Tan — there's no support team, just the person who built this.

Not financial advice

Ratings, signals, position sizing suggestions, and the Track Record shown on this site are for informational and educational purposes only. Nothing here is personalized investment advice. Market data is delayed. Trade at your own risk, and do your own research.

Changes to this policy

If what the site collects or how it's used changes meaningfully, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
